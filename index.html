How it works

Simple for members. Simple for gyms. You label machines once, and members scan to log forever.

1

Place the QR labels on your gym equipment

You provide us with your equipment list. We generate your Qr-code labels and ship them directly to you. Each label points to a machine-specific link (gym + machine ID). No app store required.

2

Member scans and logs instantly

Strength machines show sets/reps/weight. Cardio machines show time + machine stats.

3

History is always tied to the machine

Members can see what they did last time on that exact piece of equipment.

4

Download + Upload to move phones

Members can export their data to a file and re-upload it on a new device to restore it.

FAQ

Quick answers to common questions gyms and members ask.

Does this require an app download?

No. ScanLiftLog is QR-first and opens instantly in the mobile browser.

Do members need to create an account?

No personal information is required. History stays on-device by default, and users can export/import data.

What happens if a gym cancels?

Gyms can be set to ACTIVE/DISABLED. When disabled, QR scans show “service inactive” without removing stickers.
