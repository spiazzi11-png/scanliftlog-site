ScanLiftLog

A frictionless way for gym members to log workouts on any machine—no app, no accounts, no personal data. Just scan and lift.

How it works

1. Stick a QR Apply a small QR code to each machine.
2. Members scan Any phone camera opens the workout page—no app required.
3. Log instantly Sets, reps, or cardio are saved on the member’s device.

Works on all equipment. No integrations. No member accounts. No privacy risk.
