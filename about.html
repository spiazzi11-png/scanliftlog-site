About Chris Spiazzi

Know who you're working with and why ScanLiftLog was built.

Hi, I’m Chris Spiazzi, founder of ScanLiftLog.

I created ScanLiftLog because I saw an opportunity to make workout tracking easier for both gym owners and members. Too often, people want to track their progress, but most systems are either too complicated, require an app download, or create enough friction that people stop using them consistently.

ScanLiftLog solves that with a simple idea: members scan a QR code on a machine and instantly log their workout history, progress, and performance with no app, no login, and no hassle.

My background is in operations, logistics, and building systems that make things run better. I’ve always been focused on improving processes, increasing efficiency, and creating tools that people actually use. With ScanLiftLog, I’ve taken that same mindset and applied it to the gym environment.

This isn’t a generic software company or a mass-produced fitness product. ScanLiftLog is something I’ve built with a hands-on approach, paying attention to the details that matter, from gym branding and QR label design to user experience, analytics, and real-world functionality.

My goal is simple: help gyms offer a better member experience, encourage consistency, and bring a modern, engaging layer to the workout floor.

When you work with ScanLiftLog, you’re working directly with the person who built it, believes in it, and is committed to making it better.
